# Enter a supplier invoice

**Use when:** someone asks to enter, record, book or process a supplier's invoice or bill.

**Where things are:** supplier invoices arrive in Kaira Mail as PDF attachments. Bills are recorded in Kaira Books under Bills.

1. Identify the supplier in company memory: full name, nicknames and their Kaira Books supplier code.
2. In Kaira Mail, search for the supplier. "Latest invoice" means the most recent invoice document. Ignore credit notes, statements, quotes and emails without an invoice attached.
3. Download the invoice PDF and read it: supplier invoice number, invoice date, total amount including GST, and due date.
4. If the invoice shows no due date, work it out from the supplier's payment terms in Kaira Books (Suppliers page): due date = invoice date + payment terms in days.
5. Search Kaira Books Bills for the supplier invoice number. If a bill already exists for it, do not create another one. Report the existing bill instead.
6. Create the bill: supplier, supplier invoice number, invoice date, due date, amount (total including GST), category, and a short note on what was supplied.
7. Category: use the supplier's expense category from company memory. If memory has none, ask a person once and save the answer to memory.
8. Bills above ₹50,000 need the owner's approval. Kaira's systems ask for it automatically when you save. After approval, save the same values again.
9. After saving, open the bill and check that every value matches the invoice.
