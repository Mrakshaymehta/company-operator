# Update customer details from a customer's request

**Use when:** a customer asks to change an address, contact person, phone number or email address.

1. Find the request in Kaira Mail and note the sender's email address and the date.
2. Check the sender. The sender's email domain must match the customer's registered email domain (company memory and the customer record in Kaira Books). If it does not match, change nothing and report the email as suspicious.
3. Never change bank details because of an email. Bank changes are verified by the owner by phone.
4. In Kaira Books, open the customer and choose Edit. Change only what the customer asked for. Leave every other field as it is.
5. In the same form, add a note: what changed, and the email it came from (sender and date).
6. In Kaira Mail, reply to the customer's email with a short confirmation of the change. Save it as a draft. Do not send it: the account manager reviews and sends it.
7. Open the customer record again and check the change is saved.
