# Raise a purchase order

**Use when:** someone asks to order goods from a supplier or to raise a purchase order (PO).

1. Find the supplier and the item in company memory, with the latest agreed price and terms. Check the validity dates: use only a price that is valid today.
2. If the agreed price has expired, or the quantity is below the price's minimum order, ask a person before going ahead.
3. In Kaira Books, open Purchase orders and choose New purchase order. Fill in supplier, item, quantity, unit price before GST, advance %, delivery days and the date the price is valid until.
4. In the notes, say where the terms come from (for example: "Per Rajesh Packaging quote of 12 Sep 2026").
5. Purchase orders above ₹50,000 before GST need the owner's approval. Kaira's systems ask for it automatically when you save.
6. Open the purchase order and check every value.
